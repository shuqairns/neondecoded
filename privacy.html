Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers the Neon Decoded website and the private publishing tool ("the tool") that Neon Decoded uses to post its own videos.

Who uses the tool

Only the operator of the Neon Decoded channels. The tool is not available to the public and does not let anyone else sign in.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with the operator's permission, it uploads videos to the Neon Decoded channel and reads that channel's video status and analytics (views, watch time). It uses YouTube API Services.
  • Instagram (Meta): it publishes Reels to the Neon Decoded Instagram account and reads their status.
  • TikTok: it sends videos to the Neon Decoded TikTok account and reads their status.

It does not access, collect or store information about viewers, followers or any other users.

How data is stored and shared

Sign-in tokens and video statistics are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to, to perform the upload or status check the operator asked for.

Google and YouTube

By using a service that uses YouTube API Services you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

Revoking access on a platform stops the tool immediately. To have stored tokens and statistics deleted, email the address below; they are deleted within 7 days.
